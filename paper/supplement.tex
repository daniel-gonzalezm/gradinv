\begin{abstract}
This supplement gives the basis units in components, the map of the key tests, the
provenance of every input number, the compliance blocks of the data sets, the
self-consistent estimate against grain size, the iron example of Solyaev and three
routes to the averaged Eshelby tensor of the sphere. Every table, figure and number in it
is written by the scripts of the public code (\url{https://github.com/daniel-gonzalezm/gradinv}).
\end{abstract}
