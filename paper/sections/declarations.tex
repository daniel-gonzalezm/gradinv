\section*{Data availability}

The code that produces every figure, table and number of this article and of its
supplementary material, with its test suite, is the Python package \texttt{gradinv},
available under the MIT licence at \url{https://github.com/daniel-gonzalezm/gradinv} and
archived on Zenodo~\cite{GonzalezMonsalve2026gradinv}. The atomistic constants of the cubic
metals are taken from Lazar et al.~\cite{Lazar2022} and Po et al.~\cite{Po2019}; they and
every other input are recorded, with their provenance, in the file
\texttt{analysis/data/provenance.csv} (supplementary material, section~S3). The data,
tables and figures are released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence.
