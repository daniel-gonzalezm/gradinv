\section{Provenance of the input data}\label{sup:provenance}

Table~\ref{tab:s-provenance} lists every input of the paper and of this supplement, one
row per group of values. Three kinds occur: \emph{published}, transcribed from the source;
\emph{derived}, digitised by us from a published figure; and \emph{assumed}, chosen in this
work: evaluation points and grids, and the contact stiffnesses and fabric of the granular
hexagonal tensors of section~6 of the paper, taken within the range of their source. A test fails if a value of
the data files has no row. The transcribed digits were compared with the rendered pages
and, by a script of the code, with the text of the source PDFs. Computed numbers show no
more digits than the rounding of the published inputs supports: the last digit is
uncertain by at most five units, and $\dagger$ marks a relative uncertainty above 1\,\%.
